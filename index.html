Why WØM exists

Cocktail culture moves fast. Most of it disappears.

Guest shifts, takeovers, bar collaborations and cocktail events often live for a few hours inside Instagram stories, flyers and private chats. WØM makes them visible to the people who care.

—

For bars and bartenders: more visibility for real work.

—

For brands and festivals: stronger reach inside the cocktail scene.

—

For guests and professionals: a clearer view of what is happening.

Visibility with context

Not just another repost: WØM connects events, bars, bartenders, brands and cities into a wider cocktail culture story.
