WØM Media Partner Kit

Use for cocktail and hospitality events promoted with World of Mixologists.

Recommended wording on flyers:
- Media Partner: World of Mixologists
- or WØM Media Partner

Basic use:
1. Add the WØM Media Partner logo lockup to the flyer.
2. Keep clear space around the mark.
3. Do not stretch, distort, recolor, or place it on a low-contrast background.
4. Contact WØM with the event details: flyer, city, date, venue, guest bartender / guest bar, brands if relevant.
