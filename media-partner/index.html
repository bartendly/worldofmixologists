The idea

Your event should live beyond one post.

A guest shift or cocktail event often disappears inside stories, reposts and scattered flyers. WØM gives it media visibility. Bartendly gives it structure and a place in the wider calendar.

Real-world signal

A logo on the flyer means more than a tag. It makes WØM part of the event communication from the start.

Global cocktail audience

WØM helps the right people see what is happening: bartenders, bars, brands, festivals and cocktail lovers.

What you get

Media visibility for cocktail events.

When WØM is media partner on your event, we help create visibility around the event, the bar, the bartender, the city and the story behind it.

—

WØM appears on your event flyer as media partner.

—

Your event can be promoted through WØM stories, posts, weekly guest-shift round-ups or editorial content.

—

The bar, guest bartender, guest bar, brand partners and city can gain visibility.

—

The event can also live on Bartendly, so it can be found by city and date.
